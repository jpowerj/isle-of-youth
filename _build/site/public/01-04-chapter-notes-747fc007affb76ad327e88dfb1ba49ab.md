# Chapter notes

There are many excellent texts on the general topic of algorithms, including those by Aho, Hopcroft, and Ullman [@aho_design_1974; @aho_data_1983], Dasgupta, Papadimitriou, and Vazirani [@dasgupta_algorithms_2006], Edmonds [@edmonds_how_2008], Erickson [@erickson_algorithms_2019], Goodrich and Tamassia [@goodrich_algorithm_2001; @goodrich_data_2014], Kleinberg and Tardos [@kleinberg_algorithm_2006], Knuth [@knuth_art_1997], Levitin [@levitin_introduction_2014], Louridas [@louridas_realworld_2017], Mehlhorn and Sanders [@mehlhorn_algorithms_2008], Mitzenmacher and Upfal [@mitzenmacher_probability_2005], Neapolitan [@neapolitan_foundations_2014], Roughgarden [@roughgarden_algorithms_2022], Sanders, Mehlhorn, Dietzfelbinger, and Dementiev [@sanders_sequential_2019], Sedgewick and Wayne [@sedgewick_algorithms_2011], Skiena [@skiena_algorithm_2009], Soltys-Kulinicz [@soltys-kulinicz_introduction_2009], Wilf [@wilf_algorithms_2002], and Williamson and Shmoys [@williamson_design_2011].

Some of the more practical aspects of algorithm design are discussed by Bentley [@bentley_writing_1982; @bentley_more_1988; @bentley_programming_2000], Bhargava [@bhargava_grokking_2024], Kochenderfer and Wheeler [@kochenderfer_algorithms_2019], and McGeoch [@mcgeoch_guide_2012].

Surveys of the field of algorithms can also be found in books by Atallah and Blanton [@atallah_algorithms_2022] and Mehta and Sahhi [@mehta_handbook_2004].

For less technical material, see the books by Christian and Griffiths [@christian_algorithms_2017], Cormen [@cormen_algorithms_2013], Erwig [@erwig_once_2022], MacCormick [@maccormick_nine_2011], and Vo¨ cking et al. [448].
Overviews of the algorithms used in computational biology can be found in books
by Jones and Pevzner [240], Elloumi and Zomaya [134], and Marchisio [315].
